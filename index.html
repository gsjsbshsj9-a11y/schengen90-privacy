1. Summary

The app works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network connection of any kind initiated by the app.

2. Data you enter and where it is stored

To do its job the app stores, only on your device, the information you type in:

  • trip entry and exit dates, optional trip labels (for example a city name), and whether a trip is exempt from the 90/180 rule;
  • passport profiles you create (a name you choose) and, optionally, visa validity dates and the number of days a visa sticker allows;
  • app settings: language, theme, reminder preferences and whether the introduction was completed.

This data is kept in a private file inside the app's own storage area. It is protected by the operating system's app sandbox and, on iOS, by device encryption when the device is locked. The developer has no access to it. The data is deleted when you delete the app; individual trips and profiles can be deleted inside the app at any time.

3. Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect or process your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage statistics or any other personal data. It contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking.

4. Features that move data outside the app, only at your request

  • Copy report / Copy as CSV / Export backup place text on your device clipboard. Nothing leaves the device unless you paste it into another app.
  • Import backup reads the clipboard only when you tap the import action and confirm the replacement dialog.
  • Official calculator link copies a public web address (ec.europa.eu) to the clipboard; the app itself never opens web pages.

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device, generated from your own trip data. No push service and no server is involved. You can withdraw the permission at any time in the system settings. Android permissions used: POST_NOTIFICATIONS and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED. No other permissions are requested.

6. Legal basis and your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Because the app does not transmit personal data to the developer, the developer does not process your personal data within the meaning of Article 4(2) GDPR and holds no data about you. Your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection are exercised directly on your device: you can view, edit, export and delete all data inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling it. You may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in some EU member states) and collects no data from anyone.

8. Accuracy disclaimer

The app calculates the 90/180 short-stay rule (Regulation (EU) 2016/399, Art. 6(1)) from the dates you enter. Results are indicative only and do not constitute a right to enter or stay. Border authorities make the final decision.

9. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and published at this address and inside the app before the change takes effect.
